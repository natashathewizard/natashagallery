02 / Behind the paintings

The studio / Notes from the artist

Made slowly, and sometimes with a cat watching.

Some paintings begin with something I saw. Others come from feelings I can't quite explain. Sometimes it's a memory. Sometimes it's just a cat being a cat.

This is the place for sketches, unfinished things, and a look at how the work comes together. Nothing has to be perfectly resolved here.

04 / Hello, hello

I'm Natasha.
I paint things.

I'm a Malaysian painter making little worlds out of ordinary moments. Cats, dreamy people, a bit of longing, and all the things that are difficult to put into words.

My paintings can be quietly funny or a little sad. Sometimes both. I like it that way.

Welcome to my universe. Take a look around. You can stay as long as you like.
